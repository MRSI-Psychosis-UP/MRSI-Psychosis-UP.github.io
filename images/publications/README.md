# Publication thumbnails

Each publication's thumbnail is read from the path below, in `images/publications/`.
The paths are relative to the site root. Replace an image at the same path to
update its thumbnail without changing the HTML. The first two rows have no image
yet and show “Figure coming soon” until one is added at that path.

| Image path | Publication |
| --- | --- |
| `images/publications/childhood_adversity_meta_analysis.png` | Revisiting the link between childhood adversity and stress-sensitive brain regions in psychosis and bipolar disorder: a systematic review and meta-analysis |
| `images/publications/mmp9_rage.png` | MMP9/RAGE pathway overactivation mediates redox dysregulation and neuroinflammation, leading to inhibitory/excitatory imbalance: a reverse translation study in schizophrenia patients |
| `images/publications/high_res_MRSI_EC.png` | High-resolution whole-brain magnetic resonance spectroscopic imaging in youth at risk for psychosis |
| `images/publications/mrsi_connectome.png` | Constructing the human brain metabolic connectome with MR spectroscopic imaging reveals cerebral biochemical organization |
| `images/publications/systematic_review.jpg` | Relationships between adverse childhood experiences, oxidative stress, and development of mental disorders: A systematic review of animal and human studies. |
| `images/publications/zoe_nature.png` | Sex-specific interactions between stress axis and redox balance are associated with internalizing symptoms and brain white matter microstructure in adolescents. |
| `images/publications/WhiteMatterDisruptionsinSchizophrenia.jpeg` | White Matter Disruptions in Schizophrenia Are Spatially Widespread and Topologically Converge on Brain Network Hubs. |

Square images of at least 220 × 220 pixels work well. Figures keep their original
proportions and fit inside the reserved thumbnail area without being cropped.
Until an image is available, its area displays “Figure coming soon”. After adding
an image, reload the page to see it. Commit and push the image files with the site
to make them available on GitHub Pages.
